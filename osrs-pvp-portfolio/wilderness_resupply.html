Wilderness Resupply

Wilderness Resupply is a concept I wrote in my thread from 3 years ago where essentially you'd have ways to replenish your food and potions without leaving the Wilderness.

Many of my suggestions focus on this idea of being able to actually stay inside the Wilderness for an extended trip without leaving the Wilderness.

It would also be useful for PvMers and anti-PKers to keep their supplies topped up for when a PKer inevitably comes after them, because it's a common criticism that if you engage in the non-PKing parts of the Wilderness, you will be using up your supplies and be unprepared when PKers come after you.

Naturally, if longer trips in the Wilderness were to be a thing, you'd need a way to replenish your food and potions without going back to a bank.

Resupply Ideas

So here's some creative ideas I thought of for facilitating Wilderness Resupply:

  • Roaming Wilderness Rogues who drop a Rogue's Backpack containing a basic inventory of supplies. This backpack could function similarly to the "Supplies" item from Tombs of Amascut, except maybe with the inability to store supplies in it and with it falling apart when you've looted it.
  • Barehanded Fishing Spots conveniently located next to Volcanic Geysers to cook on.
  • Grab branches from any tree and start a barehanded mini-fire to cook on.
  • Steal some rations from poorly guarded crates at the Dark Warrior's fortress.
  • Maybe loot bandages from those Dark Warrior Fortress crates you can use on your teammates.
  • Wildertrees, Wilderbushes, Wilderberries in Callisto's boss room.
  • Champion Ghost or Reward Coffins at Vet'tion that give blighted supplies... or a Cursed Gold Chalice that crumbles to dust once you've drunk all its wine. And a Cursed Turkey Platter - a true meal fit for a king.
  • What would you eat from Venenatis's room? I don't wanna think about it.
  • Or I guess you could rip open one of the web cocoons and pull out the backpack of a dead adventurer, which happens to be full of blighted supplies. You know... the Rogue's Backpack.
  • Limited access supply banks around the Wilderness that you can only access when out-of-combat, which only let you access food, potions, runes, and ammo.
  • I can come up with these all day.

Generally, you would not be able to access these while in combat.

Interestingly enough, ideas similar to this were talked about a lot in the official OSRS PvP Feedback Discord, and now Wilderness Bosses, Revenants, and Wilderness Agility all actually do drop extra blighted supplies for you to be able to resupply.

So it's definitely an idea that Jagex likes.
